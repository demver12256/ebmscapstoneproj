🔍 QUICK FIX: CORS ERROR SA GOOGLE SIGN-IN
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Ang error na nakuha mo:
❌ "Access-Control-Allow-Origin header is present"

ITO AY HINDI NORMAL! Ang Google Sign-In dapat walang CORS issue.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
⚡ FASTEST SOLUTION (TRY NOW!)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Step 1: Press Ctrl+Shift+N (Incognito mode)
Step 2: Type: http://localhost:3000/login
Step 3: Click "Sign in with Google"

✅ KUNG GUMANA = Browser extension ang may problema
❌ KUNG HINDI = Network/antivirus ang may problema

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🧪 DIAGNOSTIC PAGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Open this in your browser:
http://localhost:3000/diagnose-google.html

This will show you exactly what's blocking Google Sign-In.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🔧 COMMON CAUSES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. BROWSER EXTENSIONS (Most Common!)
   ❌ uBlock Origin
   ❌ AdBlock / AdBlock Plus
   ❌ Ghostery
   ❌ Privacy Badger
   ❌ HTTPS Everywhere
   ❌ Any privacy/security extension

   FIX: Disable ALL extensions temporarily

2. ANTIVIRUS SOFTWARE
   ❌ Kaspersky
   ❌ Avast
   ❌ Norton
   ❌ Bitdefender
   ❌ Any antivirus with "Web Protection"

   FIX: Disable web protection temporarily

3. VPN/PROXY
   ❌ Any VPN connection
   ❌ Proxy server

   FIX: Disconnect VPN/proxy temporarily

4. NETWORK FIREWALL
   ❌ Corporate network
   ❌ School network
   ❌ Shared network with restrictions

   FIX: Try from different network (mobile hotspot)
